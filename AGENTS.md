## Film Contract

This repository rebuilds the two introduction films of the Data Department
Work Guidelines, one in Chinese and one in English, from its tracked sources.
Source truth, illustrative examples, technical evidence, model opinion and
human listening remain distinct. Each edition is exactly 1500 seconds of native
60 fps 2D picture with the selected narrator, 48 kHz stereo narration, small
single-line captions and no music.

The scripts, casts and picture composition are the reviewed films. Change them
only through an accepted Change that reopens review. A rebuild inherits the
published films' review only by meeting the reproduction standard in
`openspec/specs/film-reproduction`. Preserve natural voiced phrase tails and
thought-group pauses; never reach the runtime by deleting necessary
punctuation or forcing uniform speed.

Generated output belongs under the ignored `build/`. The tracked tree holds
sources only; the films are published as release assets.
